Good to know about Budapest

Overview

Budapest deserves two full sightseeing days: Buda (castle and viewpoints) and Pest (Parliament, Basilica, Christmas markets). The Vörösmarty Square market runs from 13 Nov 2026 to the New Year.

Optional experiences

Széchenyi Thermal Bath (allow 2–3 hours), New York Café (expect queues), Heroes' Square, City Park and Vajdahunyad Castle, Dohány Street Synagogue, a Danube cruise after dark.

Optional extra

Hospital in the Rock Nuclear Bunker Museum near the Castle District, good for history fans.
